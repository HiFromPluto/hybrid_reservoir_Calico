## Why this layout, not the printed 5-AC table

The plan WHY is collinear input plumes. That table was written for a
different dish: five clustered ACs, flow 8 µm/s, and a glucose AC5.
This rebuild has **four grounded sources**, `FLOW_SPEED=0`, and no
glucose / Monod / nutrient AC. x-separation survives. Copying the table
would be a FAIL of the stage.

Plume scales at the frozen decays, used to predeclare positions, not to
tune after SVD:

- attractant √(D/k) = √(100/0.0067) ≈ 122 µm
- AHL √(159/0.0033) ≈ 219 µm
- acid √(200/0.0067) ≈ 173 µm
